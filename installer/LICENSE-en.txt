1. PURPOSE

The Agent is a technical inventory and vulnerability-management support tool. It
is installed as a Windows service, periodically collects technical information
from the computer, and sends it to Firulai over HTTPS so that the inventory can
be maintained and correlated with known vulnerabilities.

5. BACKGROUND OPERATION

initial inventory, attempts to update the inventory daily, may retry failed
submissions, and stores local configuration, state, inventory, and technical
logs. It requires HTTPS connectivity to Firulai.

Uninstalling removes the local service and files, but does not automatically
delete inventory already stored in Firulai. The system may be marked as
disconnected or inactive and remote data must be managed from Firulai.
